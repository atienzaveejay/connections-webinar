Recruiters don't browse LinkedIn. They filter it.

When a recruiter has a role to fill, they don't scroll through profiles. They open LinkedIn Recruiter, type in a job title, set a location and add the skills the role needs. Anyone who falls outside those filters never shows up, however strong their experience.

Kate runs these searches for her clients. In this hour she opens one live and shows you exactly what she sees, including how fast a long list of qualified people shrinks to a short one. Then she walks you through the two settings on your own profile that decide which side of that filter you land on: your Skills and your Location.
